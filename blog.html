Inside Zanzibar's Fight to Save Its Sea Turtles

On the eastern shores of Zanzibar, a small team of local rangers and volunteers patrol the beaches at dawn, tagging nests and rescuing hatchlings from plastic-choked tide pools. We spent a week with the turtle conservation project in Jambiani to see what it really takes to protect a species that has outlived the dinosaurs — and why every rescued hatchling matters to the fishing communities who now call themselves its protectors.

The dry plains around Engaruka lose more topsoil every year to wind and overgrazing. We joined a volunteer group and local farmers digging planting holes at sunrise, and learned why choosing the right indigenous species matters as much as the number of trees in the ground.

In villages where most working-age adults have moved to town for work, elderly women are often left to care for themselves and their grandchildren alone. We visit a weekly home-visit programme that brings food, medical checks, and simply company to women who spend most days without a single guest.

Protecting the Springs: River Conservation Work in Remote Arusha

As glaciers on Kilimanjaro retreat and rainfall grows less predictable, the rivers that feed rural Arusha are under real pressure. We follow a community-led project clearing invasive plants from the riverbanks, fencing off water points from livestock, and teaching farmers how to keep the water flowing for the next generation.

How a Small Savings Group Is Changing Life for Women in Engaruka

Twelve women, one notebook, and a shared box of savings — that's how it started. Today the Engaruka women's cooperative runs a vegetable garden, a small tailoring business, and a peer-lending fund. We sat in on a Thursday meeting to understand how small, steady support is helping women build real financial independence.

Teaching Civic Rights in the Villages Where They're Rarely Discussed

Many residents in Tanzania's more remote wards have never attended a session explaining their basic legal rights, how local government works, or how to raise a grievance. We join a travelling civic education workshop that uses simple language, local dialect, and open discussion to close that gap, one village meeting at a time.

From Egg to Ocean: A Night Patrol With Zanzibar's Turtle Rangers

Green and hawksbill turtles have nested on Zanzibar's beaches for centuries, but poaching and coastal development put every clutch of eggs at risk. We join a night patrol as rangers relocate a threatened nest to a protected hatchery, and watch the quiet, careful process of giving a new generation of turtles a fighting chance.
